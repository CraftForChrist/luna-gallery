Luna Gallery gives local artists a home on Ypsilanti’s main streets and gives visitors a better way to give gifts: ones with a story, and a maker you can name.

Our shop is welcoming to everyone, and we’re proud to carry work from LGBTQ+ artists as well as artists from every part of the community.
